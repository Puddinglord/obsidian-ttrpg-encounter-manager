## The descent of Magistrix Vess

In the Year of Burning Stars, Magistrix Sylvie Vess discovered ancient formulas promising transformation of base metals into philosopher's mercury. Her research consumed her, driving her to construct these vaults beneath the city's oldest quarter.

As months passed, her letters to the Alchemists' Guild grew erratic. She wrote of quicksilver that moved with purpose and of reflections that whispered equations. The Guild dismissed her ravings, until the night silver light flooded from her laboratory's windows and all sound ceased within.

The Magistrix was never seen again, though on moonless nights, locals swear they see silver light seeping from the cracks between the cobblestones, and hear the whisper of mathematical formulas on the wind.

```box secret title="The truth"
The truth, as with all things concerning the Magistrix, flows like quicksilver through grasping fingers: beautiful, deadly, and impossible to hold.

[[Magistrix Vess|Vess]] is alive, after a fashion. She stands in the [[Q7 Sacred Circle]], half flesh and half mercury, finishing the work. She means to pour herself into the city's foundations and make all of it liquid and perfect.
```

## Hooks

```box quest title="Ways in"
- **The Guild's quiet job.** [[Tobren Kask]], master assayer of the Alchemists' Guild, pays 60 gp a head to go down, find out what Vess became, and bring back her research notes. The Guild would rather nobody knew it ignored her letters.
- **The thief who didn't come back.** [[Wick]] went into the vaults two nights ago after the Magistrix's silver. His fence will pay 40 gp to anyone who brings him out, and more for the silver.
- **The enforcer.** [[Sister Odile Varr]] has orders to make sure nothing in the vaults ever leaves them. She'd rather go down with help, and she isn't picky about whose.
```

## Running it

- **Map:** [[Map - The Quicksilver Vaults]]. Areas Q1 to Q7 follow the numbers on the map.
- **The route:** the stair comes down into Q1. Q2, the Mercury Pool, is the hub. The east passage leads to the lab (Q4), the living quarters (Q5) and storage (Q6). The west passage leads past the Calcinator (Q3) to the Sacred Circle (Q7).
- **Fights:** six encounter blocks for four level 2 heroes. Most are Easy on paper, but the constructs and the ooze split, so they hit harder than they rate. The finale is Very Deadly if the Mercury Elemental fights from the start, and Hard if you hold it back until the Alchemist is Bloodied.
- **Mercury everywhere:** the vapour, the pool and the hounds' bites all call for **STR saves**. Have the heroes keep track of who's breathed the most.

- Treasure in Q2, Q3, Q4, Q5 and Q6, and the **Quicksilver Font** in [[Q7 Sacred Circle]].
