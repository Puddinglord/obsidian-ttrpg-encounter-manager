```box read-aloud
As the door opens, the Alchemist hovers inches above a circle of silver inlay, her robes rippling without wind. Mercury flows upward from the circle's channels, orbiting her like liquid moons. She turns. Her flesh has the sheen of polished metal, and quicksilver tears flow upward from her eyes.
```

*"Perfect timing,"* she says, her voice resonating like fingers on crystal. *"More material for the final transformation."*

![[The Alchemist.png|300]]

A perfect circle of silver inlay dominates the floor, mathematical formulas and alchemical symbols worked into its design. Brass censers hang from chains, still smoking with sweet-smelling incense. The walls continuously whisper mathematical formulas, growing louder as anyone approaches the circle's center.

```box secret title="Breaking the circle"
A hero who knows where the inlay's deliberate break is (from [[Q3 The Calcinator]] or Vess's notes) can scrape it through as an action with a **Might** check (DC 12). While the circle is broken, the Alchemist is Dazed at the start of each of her turns and can't use Quicksilver Binding.
```

```box treasure title="The Quicksilver Font"
When the Alchemist falls, the mercury in the circle settles into a still, bright font. Heroes can dip their weapons into it to give them the **Quicksilver** enchantment: when the weapon hits, the target makes a DEX save (DC 8) or its own weapon damage die drops one size (minimum d4) until the end of the fight.
```

```box exits
- **North:** [[Q3 The Calcinator]].
```
