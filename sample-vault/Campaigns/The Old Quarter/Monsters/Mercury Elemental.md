The purest form of Vess's philosophical mercury, this entity represents her closest success at creating truly living metal. Neither fully elemental nor entirely artificial, it possesses a cruel intelligence and serves as both guardian and proof of concept for her theories of metallic transcendence.

```nimble
name: Mercury Elemental
level: 3
size: Large
type: Elemental
hp: 40
armor: none
movement: 6
saves: STR+ DEX+
attacks:
- **Slam (2×).** 1d10+2. On a hit: STR save DC 12 or take 1d6 more poison damage.
abilities:
- Liquid Form: It can flow through any gap an inch wide without slowing down.
- Fire Thickens It: After it takes fire damage, it's Slowed until the end of its next turn.
group: Vess's creations
habitat: The Quicksilver Vaults
easier: Only one Slam a turn.
harder: Poison damage on every hit, no save.
```
