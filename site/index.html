Free, open-source Obsidian plugin for Nimble GMs

A GM toolkit as nimble as you are.

Manage your campaign, encounters, NPCs, dice rolls and more in Obsidian, so you spend more time at the table and less time in your notes.

Less looking things up. More playing.

1

Build a fight in one line

Type /encounter 4 quicksilver hound. You get a block that rates the fight against your party, from Easy to Very Deadly, and suggests tweaks to hit the difficulty you want.

2

Let it do the math

Tap a monster's HP. The calculator applies crits, armor, vulnerabilities and minion overkill, and shows you the sum before you commit. Bloodied and Last Stand pop up when they happen.

3

Stat blocks pop up over your note

Click any monster or NPC and their sheet opens over your note, with HP, turn and actions. Close it and you're right where you left off.

Powered by the simplicity of Markdown.

Your adventure, your NPCs and your homebrew monsters are text files in a folder you control. Ember Vault adds the tools on top. Turn the plugin off and every note is still there, still readable.

  • Runs on your device with nothing to sign up for
  • Works offline, at the table or on the train
  • Syncs however you already sync Obsidian
  • Write your own monsters as ordinary notes

Install in about a minute

  1. Open Obsidian and go to Settings → Community plugins → Browse.
  2. Search for Ember Vault, then click Install and Enable.
  3. Add your party in Settings → Ember Vault, then type /encounter in any note.
