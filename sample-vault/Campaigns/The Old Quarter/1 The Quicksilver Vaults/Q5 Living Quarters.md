```box read-aloud
A personal chamber turned to madness. Moldering books and scattered notes lie everywhere. A brass bed frame has melted partially into the floor.
```

Mathematical formulas and prayers are scratched into every surface, culminating in a ritual circle carved around the bed. **A Mercury Ooze** hides in the melted brass, and rises when anyone touches the bed or the notes.

```nimble-encounter

notes: The first time it drops to 10 HP or less it splits into two oozes. Use Duplicate in the tracker's ⋯ menu, then set both to half its HP. It goes for whoever has the most metal on them.
```
