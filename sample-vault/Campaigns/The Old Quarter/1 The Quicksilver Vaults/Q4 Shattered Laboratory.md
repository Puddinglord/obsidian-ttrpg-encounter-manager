```box read-aloud
Broken glass and twisted metal cover the floor. Tilted workbenches hold the remains of a complex alchemical apparatus. Cracked mirrors line the walls, each reflection slightly different from reality.
```

![[Alchemy bench.png]]

[[Wick]] is hiding here, under the fallen cabinet in the corner, with a hound bite on his leg that won't stop shaking. He's been listening to the mirrors for two days.

```box trap title="Glass shards"
**Trigger:** anyone touching a workbench. The broken glass animates and swirls.
**Spot:** Perception DC 13. A shard lifts a hair's width off the floor when someone leans close.
**Effect:** DEX save DC 15 or take 2d6 damage.
**Disarm:** don't touch the benches. Clever thinking works too: knocking an apparatus off with a pole, or throwing a cloak over the glass first.
```

```box look title="The mirrors"
Each mirror shows the room a few seconds ahead. A hero who watches one for a minute (**Insight**, DC 12) sees themselves touch the workbench, and the glass rise, before it happens.
```

```box treasure
**Intact alchemist's kit** (250 gp), hidden under the fallen cabinet. Wick is lying on it.
```
