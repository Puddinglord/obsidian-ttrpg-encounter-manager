```box quest title="The hook"
[[Tobren Kask]] pays 60 gp a head, half up front, to go down into the vaults under the Old Quarter, find out what Magistrix Vess became, and bring back her research notes. He'd rather nobody knew the Guild ignored her letters.
```

## Objectives
- [ ] Get into the vaults
- [ ] Find out what happened to Vess
- [ ] Bring back her research notes (in [[Q5 Living Quarters]])

## Stakes
- **If they succeed:** the Guild owes them, and Kask pays the second half without a fuss.
- **If they fail or walk away:** Vess finishes the work in [[Q7 Sacred Circle]] and the Old Quarter starts to turn to mercury, one street at a time.

## The truth
*What's really going on, that the heroes don't know yet.*
Vess wrote to the Guild for months and the Guild ignored her. Kask wants the notes back before anyone else reads them and works out what the Guild was told, and when.

## Clues & leads
- [[Sister Odile Varr]] has seen the letters' seals. She'll mention them if anyone asks why the Guild cares.
- Vess's notes name the Alchemist's weakness: broken silver inlay.

## People & places
- [[Tobren Kask]], [[Magistrix Vess]], [[Sister Odile Varr]]
- [[The Alchemists' Guild]], [[1 The Quicksilver Vaults]]

## Complications
- Odile has orders that nothing leaves the vaults, notes included.
- [[Wick]] has already pocketed one of the letters.

## Rewards
- 60 gp each, half up front.

## Progress
- Kask makes the offer at the start of [[Session 1 - Silver Between the Stones]].
