A GM toolkit as nimble as you are.

Manage your campaign, encounters, monsters, quests, characters and dice in Obsidian, so you spend more time at the table and less time in your notes.

Less looking things up. More playing.

1

Build a fight in one line

Type /encounter 4 quicksilver hound. You get a block that rates the fight against your party, from Easy to Very Deadly, and suggests tweaks to hit the difficulty you want.

2

Let it do the math

Tap a monster's HP. The calculator applies crits, armor, vulnerabilities and minion overkill, and shows you the sum before you commit. Bloodied and Last Stand pop up when they happen.

3

Stat blocks pop up over your note

Click any monster or NPC and their sheet opens over your note, with HP, turn and actions. Close it and you're right where you left off.

New

Play 5e too, 2014 or 2024

Give a campaign the 5e rules: both SRD bestiaries, encounters rated by your edition, an initiative tracker, the compendium, character sheets with a New character wizard, and a builder for your own monsters, spells and items.

New

5e stat blocks, both layouts

Write a ```5e block, or copy one of 675 SRD monsters, and roll everything in it.

New

A 5e sheet worth playing from

Portrait, hit points with a keypad, abilities with their skills, spells by level with their slots. Every number rolls.

New

An initiative tracker that fits the fight

A card for whoever acts, then who's still to act this round. Identical monsters share a turn, anyone can act out of turn, and legendary actions come when they can.

New

Conditions that keep time

A condition can last some rounds, end on a save, or burn each turn. The tracker asks when it's time. Make your own on the spot.

New

Bring your own content

Content packs add your table's homebrew and what can't ship, in Nimble and 5e: a new class, a book's subclasses, a third-party bestiary. Import a D&D Beyond character (its class, subclass, feats and spells join your compendium) or a Fantasy Statblocks creature.

Monster, spell and item builder

Pick a level and press Create now for a monster on the GM Guide's numbers, or type your own and see what level they play like. Spells and items follow the Creator's Kit.

New

Your own content, in the compendium

Make a class, subclass, feat, species, ancestry or background from its compendium tab: a builder takes you through it, level by level, in Nimble and 5e. Or type it as a block. It's tagged Homebrew and works in New character, Level up and the sheet like the books' own.

The whole compendium

Monsters, spells, items, features, classes and rules. Typed filters, sorting, and typos forgiven.

Character sheets that roll

Tap a stat to roll it, tap HP to take a hit. Rests, level-ups and class dice follow the rules.

New

Dice from the table

Rolled it for real? Tap an empty slot and the face you rolled. Fury, Judgment and Combat Dice go straight onto the sheet.

For players too

Set a campaign to I play in it: lore cards instead of stat blocks, battle notes, and your party up front.

New

Quests, places and sessions on cards

A quest shows who gave it, the reward, objectives you tick and the GM's secret. Places show who's there, and sessions which quests moved.

Links that say what they are

A link to a quest, place or hero shows as a pill with its icon, so a session log reads at a glance.

Campaigns that stay tidy

Chapters, scenes, NPCs, quests and sessions in one panel. Drag to reorganise, and step scene to scene at the table.

Maps with pins

A pin on every room, linked to its note. Drag fighters on as tokens.

NPCs with faces

Portrait cards with their faction, where to find them and how they feel about the party, a secret you reveal when nobody's looking, and the numbers if it comes to a fight.

Read-aloud boxes

Read-aloud text, traps, treasure and clues, each styled so you spot them mid-session.

Dice, two ways

Quick results in the corner, or 3D dice across your notes. Try both.

Your dice, your look

Nine finishes from resin to lava, your own colours in the Dice Workshop, and Dice So Nice and Rollsmith sets.

Type a slash, get a tool

Encounters, monsters, quests, NPCs, rolls and boxes, written where you're typing.

Stat blocks that help

Write your own and it forgives capitals and bold. When a line can't be read, it says why and offers a fix.

Tougher or weaker in a click

Shift any monster a level up or down. HP and dice move to the GM Guide's numbers for the new level.

Phone and iPad

Run the fight from a tablet at the table and keep your sheet on your phone. Every view is laid out for touch, and your vault syncs however Obsidian does.

Install it from Obsidian

  1. Open Obsidian and go to Settings → Community plugins → Browse.
  2. Search for Ember Vault, then click Install and Enable.
  3. Add your party in Settings → Ember Vault, then type /encounter in any note.
