# Start here

This vault holds a short, ready-to-run Nimble adventure, **The Quicksilver Vaults**, for four level 2 heroes. It's also a tour of the Nimble GM plugin: every feature is used somewhere in it. Work through the list below and you'll have seen them all.

> When Obsidian asks whether you trust this vault, choose **Trust author and enable plugins**. That turns on Nimble GM, which is already installed here.

## A ten-minute tour

1. **Open the Campaign panel.** Click the castle icon in the left ribbon. You'll see the party (Brakka, Wren, Ilse and Tomas), the chapter and its seven areas, and the NPCs with their portraits.
2. **Read the chapter.** Open [[1 The Quicksilver Vaults]] for the story of Magistrix Vess and the hooks.
3. **Open the map.** [[Map - The Quicksilver Vaults]] has a pin on every area. Hover a pin to preview it, click to open it. Scroll to zoom.
4. **Go to the first area.** [[Q1 Entry Chamber]] has a read-aloud box, a checks table and an encounter block. The block shows how hard the fight is for this party.
5. **Hover a monster.** Point at **Quicksilver Construct** in the encounter block to see its stat block. All of this adventure's monsters are notes in the Monsters folder, written as `nimble` stat blocks.
6. **Run the fight.** Click **Run** on the encounter block in Q1. The tracker opens on the right with the constructs and the heroes. Click **Start combat**, click a construct's HP and type `8` to hit it, or `8!` for a crit.
7. **Roll some dice.** Click any dice expression, like the 1d6 in the trap in [[Q2 Mercury Pool]].
8. **Meet an NPC.** Open [[Tobren Kask]]. Click the blurred **Secret** row to reveal it, then click his portrait to enlarge it.
9. **Fight the boss.** [[Q7 Sacred Circle]] has a legendary monster. After each hero's turn, the tracker shows her choices.
10. **Type `/`** at the start of any line to see every Nimble command.

## Make it yours

- Replace the party with your own heroes from the Campaign panel.
- Add an NPC with the **+** next to NPCs. It comes with an empty portrait slot: click it to upload art.
- Start your own campaign with **New campaign** in the Campaign panel. This one can stay as a reference.

The full guide is on GitHub: [Nimble GM user guide](https://github.com/Puddinglord/obsidian-ttrpg-encounter-manager/tree/main/docs).
