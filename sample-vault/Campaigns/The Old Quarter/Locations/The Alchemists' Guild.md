A tall, soot-stained hall on the edge of the old quarter, all copper roofs and locked doors. Apprentices hurry in and out with covered trays and don't make eye contact.

- **The assay hall:** where [[Tobren Kask]] works, and where he meets the heroes.
- **The letter room:** a hundred pigeonholes. One of them is stuffed with Magistrix Vess's unanswered letters.

```box rumor
- "On moonless nights you can see silver light coming up between the cobblestones."
- "The Guild sealed a cellar on Lantern Row last year. Bricked it up from the outside."
- "There's a thief who went down there. Feathered hat. Nobody's seen him since."
- "If you listen at the drains at midnight, they count."
```

## Connections
- The vaults' stair goes down from a bricked-up cellar on Lantern Row, ten minutes' walk away. See [[Q1 Entry Chamber]].
