Failed experiments where mercury was used to preserve dying guard dogs. The process left them as much liquid as solid, their loyalty twisted but intact. They hunt in packs through the vaults, their mercury-infused bodies allowing them to slip through the smallest cracks to corner their prey.

```nimble
name: Quicksilver Hound
level: 1
size: Medium
type: Construct (beast)
hp: 16
armor: none
movement: 8
saves: DEX+
attacks:
- **Bite.** 1d6+2. On a hit: STR save DC 12 or Slowed for 1d4 rounds.
abilities:
- Pack Tactics: Deals +2 damage to a target that has another hound next to it.
- Slip Away: When it's hit, it can flow up to 4 spaces away (once per round).
vulnerabilities:
- Cold
group: Vess's failures
habitat: The Quicksilver Vaults
easier: No Slip Away.
harder: Six hounds, and they open with Slip Away to surround the heroes.
```
