```box read-aloud
A knee-deep pool of living quicksilver dominates the room. The liquid metal shifts and ripples without any breeze, occasionally forming into almost-recognizable shapes before collapsing.
```

```box trap title="Metallic vapour"
**Trigger:** breathing the air here for more than a minute, or wading into the pool.
**Spot:** automatic; the air tastes of pennies.
**Effect:** STR save DC 12 or take 1d6 damage. Repeat once each minute a hero stays.
**Disarm:** a wet cloth over the mouth gives advantage on the save.
```

Three stone channels branch out from the pool, each controlled by an ancient valve mechanism. They lead west (toward [[Q3 The Calcinator]]), east (toward [[Q4 Shattered Laboratory]]) and south into the rock.

```box checks title="The valves"
| Check | DC | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Examination | 12 | Opening the south valve drains the pool into the rock in a minute. The ring at the bottom is easy to reach, and the vapour stops. |
| Examination | 12 | Opening the west valve sends mercury down the channel toward the Sacred Circle. The Alchemist gains 1 extra HP per hero in her fight for each minute it runs. |
| Might | 10 | Forcing a stuck valve. On a failure the valve snaps and sprays the hero (1d6, no save). |
```

```box treasure
**Silver ring** (300 gp) glints beneath the mercury's surface. Fishing it out of the full pool means a STR save (DC 12) for the vapour, and a DEX save (DC 10) to keep hold of it.
```

```box exits
- **North:** [[Q1 Entry Chamber]].
- **West passage:** around to [[Q3 The Calcinator]].
- **East passage:** to [[Q4 Shattered Laboratory]].
```
